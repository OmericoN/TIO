TIO Prototype Terms

Version 1

TIO is a prototype for personalized audio news. Briefings may use synthetic voices and may contain mistakes. Check the original reporting before relying on a briefing. Briefings are for general information and do not provide investment advice.

TIO uses Supabase Auth to manage your account. Your onboarding answers are stored with your profile and are intended to personalize news recommendations. If you enable the microphone in a generated briefing, ElevenLabs processes your audio to transcribe your question. Briefing audio, transcripts and citations are saved in this browser. You can delete them from Briefings.

Use your own email address and keep your password private. Use the microphone only with the consent of anyone whose voice may be captured.
