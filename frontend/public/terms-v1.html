TIO Prototype Terms

Version 1

TIO is a prototype for personalized audio news. Briefings may use synthetic voices and may contain mistakes. Check the original reporting before relying on a briefing. Briefings are for general information and do not provide investment advice.

TIO uses Supabase Auth to manage your account. Your onboarding answers are stored with your profile and are intended to personalize news recommendations. If you start the live host preview and grant microphone access, ElevenLabs processes your audio to provide the conversation.

Use your own email address and keep your password private. Use the live host preview only with the consent of anyone whose voice may be captured.
